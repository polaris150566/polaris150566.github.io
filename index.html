About

Education, toolbox, and everything else.

I am an undergraduate at the University of Science and Technology of China, majoring in Biosciences and graduating in June 2027.

My research is on deep generative models for protein and RNA design, centered on the inverse folding problem: given a 3D backbone, which sequence will actually fold into it and function in its native environment? I am particularly drawn to membrane proteins and GPCRs, where models trained on soluble proteins break down because the backbone they are handed is out of distribution.

I am currently a research intern with Prof. Yu Li at CUHK, and I work with Prof. Haiyan Liu and Prof. Quan Chen at the MOE Key Laboratory for Membraneless Organelles and Cellular Dynamics at USTC.

I am preparing to apply for a Ph.D. in machine learning for structural biology.
