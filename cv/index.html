Backbone-based Membrane Protein Sequence Design

Independent Project · National College Students' Innovation Program

Apr 2025 – May 2026

  • Fine-tuned the inverse-folding model ABACUS-T for membrane protein sequence design, closing the distribution gap that makes soluble-protein models generate hydrophilic sequences in transmembrane regions.
  • Built a training set from PDBTM using TMDET 4.0 for membrane-position annotation; encoded membrane depth via radial basis functions and reweighted the loss toward transmembrane features.
  • Improved sequence recovery from 37.87% to 40.01% on 129 independent test proteins (Wilcoxon signed-rank p < 0.001), with the largest gain at the membrane–water interface (26.7% → 30.5%).
  • Compared background prior injection, W/Y loss weighting, CFG, ControlNet-style branches, adaLN, and cross-attention; diagnosed why weak continuous geometric conditioning fails and redirected the work toward output-logit gating.

Targeted Inhibition of Tumor Angiogenesis via Surface Display of VEGF Antibodies

Team Leader · USTC 2024 iGEM

Mar 2024 – Oct 2024

  • Engineered Escherichia coli Nissle 1917 to display VEGF-binding proteins and inhibit tumor angiogenesis.
  • Screened four surface-display systems using mCherry fusions and confocal microscopy, and validated VEGF-binding capacity by ELISA.
  • Designed an MMP-responsive masking peptide so the binder activates only within the tumor microenvironment, reducing off-target risk.
  • Built a finite element model in Python for antigen-antibody interaction kinetics; predictions were consistent with experimental ELISA results.
