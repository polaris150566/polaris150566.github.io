Statistical Energy

Statistical energy functions for design evaluation

A Mixture Density Network that predicts statistical energy functions, used to evaluate and screen de novo protein designs.

Sep. 2025 – present USTC MOE Key Laboratory for Membraneless Organelles and Cellular Dynamics

The problem

Generating candidate protein designs is now comparatively easy; deciding which ones are worth synthesizing is not. A scoring function needs to separate designs that will fold from designs that merely look plausible to the generator that produced them — and it has to do so without running an experiment.

Model

I am developing a Mixture Density Network that predicts statistical energy functions for a given structure. Rather than outputting a single scalar, an MDN predicts a full distribution, which matters here because residue interactions are genuinely multi-modal — several distinct geometries can be equally favorable, and a model forced to average them would score all of them poorly.

An RNN predicts pairwise residue energies autoregressively, so each prediction is conditioned on the ones already made rather than treated as independent.

Data pipeline

Most of the engineering effort went here, since the model is only as good as its negative examples:

  • Processed the SAbDab and PPI structural datasets
  • Regenerated and optimized side-chain conformations with ABACUS-T-Ab (an internally developed ABACUS-T variant) and ProteinMPNN
  • Used AlphaFold 3 for structure prediction to generate decoys
  • Built positive and negative samples from pre- and post-redesign structures, then encoded them into pairwise features for training and evaluation

The design of this pipeline is the substance of the project: the discriminative task is defined entirely by what counts as a negative, and decoys generated by redesign plus structure prediction are much harder — and therefore more useful — than randomly perturbed structures.
